These terms are an agreement between you and BuildHQ, trading as Connect Power. By creating an account or using the Connect Power app, USSD service or a Connect Power meter, you agree to them. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

1. The service

Connect Power provides prepaid, metered electricity through smart meters, a wallet you fund by bank transfer, plans you create to control how much each meter may spend, usage analytics and AI energy insights. Meter readings come from the Switch Electric metering network.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or over and give true details. You sign in with a code sent to your email; keep access to that email and your phone safe and never share codes.
  • To get a dedicated account number you must verify your phone and identity (BVN and bank account) through Paystack.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.

3. Meters

  • A meter is linked to your account by Connect Power. You must have the right to use the supply at that place.
  • Do not tamper with, bypass, move or open a meter or its seals. Tampering is a serious offence; we may end the service and recover costs.
  • Meter readings are the basis of charging. If you think a reading is wrong, open a support request; we will check it against the network's records.

4. Wallet, price and plans

  • Price: energy is charged per kWh at the price shown in the app for your meter's site. We may change the price; a new price applies only to energy used after it takes effect, and we show it in the app beforehand.
  • Plans: you decide how much a meter may spend: a set amount from your wallet or your whole balance. You are charged only for the energy the meter uses, and never more than the plan you set. When the plan is used up, power to that meter is switched off until you start a new plan. You can stop a plan at any time.
  • Auto-renew: if you turn it on, a new plan of the same amount starts when the last one is used up, if your wallet can cover it.
  • The wallet holds money only for using Connect Power. It does not earn interest. See Wallet and refunds.

7. AI energy insights
