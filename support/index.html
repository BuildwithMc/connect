Common questions

My power is off

Check that the meter has a plan running and your wallet has money. If both are fine, open a request and tell us the meter's name.

I sent money but my wallet did not change

Transfers usually arrive in about a minute. If it has been more than 24 hours, send us the time, amount and your bank's reference.

Can a meter spend more than my plan?

No. You are charged only for energy used and never more than the plan you set.
