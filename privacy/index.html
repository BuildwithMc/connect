Your data

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data BuildHQ, trading as Connect Power ("Connect Power", "we") collects when you use the Connect Power app, the USSD service, this website and our meters, why we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the rules of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Who is responsible

BuildHQ, trading as Connect Power, No 3 Kenechukwu Chime Close, Trans-Ekulu, Enugu, Nigeria, is the data controller. Contact us about your data at info@buildhq.xyz or +234 802 601 6804.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, name, business name if you give one, and phone number.
  • Identity details for your account number: your BVN, bank account number and bank, which go straight to our payment partner Paystack to confirm your identity and give you a dedicated account number. We do not store your BVN. We keep the result of the check and the account number Paystack gives you.
  • Meter data: readings from meters linked to your account (energy used, power, voltage, current, power factor, frequency, the meter's on/off state, times) and the nickname you give each meter.
  • Money: wallet top-ups, plans you create, energy charges, refunds and adjustments, with dates and amounts. We do not see or store your bank card or online banking details.
  • Support and messages: what you write to us, and the notifications we send you.
  • Device data: a push-notification token for your phone, and basic technical logs (IP address, time, the request made) to keep the service secure.
  • Questions to the energy adviser: questions you type into the AI energy insights feature.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the service (contract): sign-in, linking your meters, your wallet and plans, charging only for the energy used and never past your plan, receipts, and support.
  • To meet legal duties (legal obligation): identity checks required for payment accounts in Nigeria, financial record keeping, tax, and answering lawful requests from authorities.
  • To keep the service safe and working (legitimate interests): fraud prevention, security logs, fixing faults, and understanding how the network is used, in a way that does not override your rights.
  • With your consent: push notifications (you can turn them off on your phone) and optional marketing messages (you can opt out at any time).

AI energy insights

When you ask for insights or ask a question about your energy, we send your meter's figures (usage, cost at our price, times, supply quality, plan status) and the meter's nickname to our AI provider, Groq, to write the answer. We do not send your name, email, phone, BVN, bank details or wallet balance. Answers are written by AI and may be imperfect; the figures shown in the app come from your meter.

Who we share data with

We share only what each partner needs, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Paystack (payments, identity checks and your dedicated account number).
  • Switch Electric (the metering network that reads your meter and sends us its data).
  • Supabase (database, sign-in and hosting).
  • eBulkSMS (sending phone verification codes) and Zoho Mail (sending emails).
  • Groq (AI energy insights, meter figures only) and Expo (delivering push notifications).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

International transfers

Some of these partners store or process data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows, such as contractual commitments to protect the data to a standard comparable to Nigerian law.

How long we keep it

  • Detailed meter readings: 90 days. Hourly energy history: while your account is open, then up to 6 years as part of billing records.
  • Wallet, plans and payments: at least 6 years after the transaction, as financial records the law requires.
  • Support messages: 2 years after the request is closed.
  • Security logs: up to 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it (see Delete your account), restrict or object to how we use it, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. In the app, More › Download my data gives you a copy straight away, and Wallet gives PDF statements and receipts. For anything else, email info@buildhq.xyz; we answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to staff who need it, every staff change is recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited, and payment details stay with Paystack. No system is perfectly secure: tell us at once at info@buildhq.xyz if you think your account has been misused.

Children

Connect Power is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Cookies

This website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. The staff dashboard stores a sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Changes

We will post changes here and tell you in the app before important changes take effect.
