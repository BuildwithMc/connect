The problem

Generators and guesswork drain your money.

When the grid goes, homes and businesses run petrol generators that eat up to 35–40% of a day's earnings, and estimated bills charge for power nobody used. Most people cannot afford solar panels and batteries of their own.

How it works

One supply, shared by everyone on it.

  1. 1

    A shared, reliable supply

    Solar panels, batteries and inverters for an estate, a market or a building, run by Connect Power. Nothing to buy, nothing to maintain.

  2. 2

    A smart meter on every home or shop

    Built to handle freezers, pumps and blenders. It measures every kilowatt-hour, so you pay for exactly what you use, never an estimate.

  3. 3

    Dial or tap to set your plan

    Dial *384*04227# on any phone, or use the app. Choose how much this meter may spend from your wallet, and your power is on in seconds.

Plans

You set the plan. You never pay past it.

Fund your wallet by bank transfer, then decide how much each meter may spend. You are charged only for the energy the meter uses, at one clear price per kWh, and never more than the plan you set.

The app

See where every naira goes.

Everything the USSD does, plus analytics for each meter, AI energy insights, your receipts and your own bank account number for topping up.

  • Your own account number. Send money from any bank app; it lands in your wallet in about a minute.
  • Your plan, your limit. Set an amount or use your whole balance. A meter never spends more than you allowed.
  • Analytics that help you decide. When you use the most, what is always on and what it costs, and how long your plan will last.
  • AI energy insights. Plain-language findings from your meter's data, and answers when you ask "why was Tuesday so high?"
  • Turn on and off. One tap on the sun switches your meter.
  • Auto-renew. Your plan starts again when it runs out, so the freezer or the pump never stops.
  • Usage and receipts. Hour by hour, day by day, with a receipt for every naira.
