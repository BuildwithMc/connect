The problem

Generators eat the profit of a market day.

When the grid goes, fish and meat spoil in hours. Traders run small petrol generators that cost them up to 35–40% of what they earn each day, and most cannot afford solar panels and batteries of their own.

How it works

One sun, shared by the whole market.

  1. 1

    A shared solar hub

    We install commercial solar panels, industrial batteries and inverters right inside the market cluster. Nothing to buy, nothing to maintain.

  2. 2

    A smart meter on every stall

    A low-cost meter built to handle freezers and blenders. It keeps your paid time in memory, so you never lose it if the network flickers.

  3. 3

    Dial or tap to turn on

    Dial *384*04227# on any phone, or use the app. Pick your hours, pay from your wallet, and your power is on in seconds.

The app

For traders with a smartphone.

Everything the USSD does, plus your usage, your receipts and your own bank account number for topping up.

  • Your own account number. Send money from any bank app; it lands in your wallet in about a minute.
  • Hours of light, not kWh. See your balance as the time it will keep your stall on.
  • Turn on and off. One tap on the sun switches your stall's power.
  • Auto-renew. Your plan starts again before it runs out, so the freezer never stops.
  • Usage and receipts. Hour by hour, day by day, with a receipt for every naira.
