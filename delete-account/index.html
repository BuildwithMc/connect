Delete your account

Last updated 23 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your Connect Power account and personal data at any time.

How to ask

  1. In the app: go to More → Account → Delete my account.
  2. By email: write to info@buildhq.xyz from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account".

What happens next

  • Your running plans stop at once, so nothing more is charged.
  • We refund your remaining wallet balance to a Nigerian bank account in your own name.
  • Within 30 days we delete your profile, phone number, meter nicknames, notifications, support messages and device tokens, and we unlink your meters.
  • What we must keep: records of payments, charges and refunds for at least 6 years, as Nigerian law requires for financial records. These are kept securely and used for nothing else.
