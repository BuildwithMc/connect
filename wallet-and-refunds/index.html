Wallet and refunds

Last updated 23 November 2026

Topping up

After you verify your identity, you get a dedicated account number from Paystack. Send money to it from any Nigerian bank app; it reaches your wallet in about a minute and you get a receipt. Transfers are processed by Paystack. Your wallet receives the full amount you send.

How money leaves your wallet

  • Only for energy your meters actually use, at the price per kWh shown in the app, and never past the plan you set for each meter.
  • For bills you choose to pay in the app (such as airtime or data), when that feature is available.
  • Every charge appears in your wallet history with a receipt.

Refunds

  • Wrong charges: if a meter reading or charge is wrong, open a support request. If we confirm the error, we credit your wallet. Corrections need approval by two members of our finance team.
  • Closing your account: we refund your remaining wallet balance to a Nigerian bank account in your own name, usually within 10 working days of confirming your request.
  • Failed transfers in: if you sent money and it has not arrived after 24 hours, contact us with the time, amount and your bank's reference.

What the wallet is not

The wallet is a prepaid balance for Connect Power services. It is not a bank account, it does not earn interest, and money cannot be sent from it to other people.
